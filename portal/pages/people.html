What the columns count
  • Commits, +/-: non-merge commits on any branch and the lines they add and delete.
  • Pushes: pushes made by this GitHub login (webhooks and Events API).
  • Broke CI: commits by this person on a default branch where a check that passed on the previous commit failed. Open = still failing.
  • Findings: review-bot findings on lines this person wrote (git blame), by severity. Slop = findings in the slop category.
  • Per 1k lines: findings weighted nit 0.5, minor 1, major 3, critical 8, per 1,000 added lines.
  • Sonar: open SonarQube issues blamed to this person: reliability, security, maintainability.
